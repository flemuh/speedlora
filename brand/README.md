# Speedlora™ Brand Assets

This directory contains the official public brand assets for Speedlora™.

## Official assets

### Logo

`speedlora-logo.svg`

The primary Speedlora logo and wordmark.

This asset defines the core visual identity of the Speedlora brand.

---

### Hero

`speedlora-hero.png`

The official Speedlora hero concept.

It represents the visual direction of the Speedlora platform, combining artificial intelligence, research, discovery, speed, and knowledge exploration.

The interface elements, metrics, and visualizations shown in this concept are illustrative and may not represent production functionality or actual product metrics.

---

### Character

`speedlora-character.png`

The official Speedlora character concept.

The character represents the human-centered side of the Speedlora identity and is designed around the themes of research, artificial intelligence, exploration, and intelligent discovery.

---
